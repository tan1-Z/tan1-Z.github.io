# Design Reference

The academic homepage layout and palette are adapted from and inspired by
[Anleeno Academic](https://github.com/Anleeno/anleeno-academic).
Its personal content, photographs, illustrations and paper figures have not been reused.
The homepage continues to use Pei Tan's own profile, publications and media.
